08 / Natural language processing · Research brief

Does the skill signal help?

Classifying a job and explaining its requirements are different tasks. This study tests a Skill + Knowledge phrase lexicon against a strong text baseline, then evaluates extraction boundaries separately.

Measured conclusion. This equal-weight lexicon fusion does not meet the study’s criterion for a meaningful improvement. The current fusion-minus-text difference is -0.0059 Macro-F1, with employer-cluster 95% interval [-0.0176, +0.0070]. The recorded study threshold is +0.02. An inconclusive interval is not proof of zero effect.

01 · Define the decision before the model

A job platform needs a reliable taxonomy; a careers service needs interpretable requirements. A dictionary can match common terms without adding category information, and a correct category does not validate an extracted skills profile.

Use the original employer-isolated train/validation/test partitions. Fit vocabulary and representations on training data, select regularization on validation data, and compare the three feature sets on the same test postings. The +0.02 threshold exists in the supplied code; no independently timestamped preregistration was provided.

02 · Evaluate explanations directly

Skill and Knowledge are separate BIO annotation channels. Exact-span scoring requires both boundaries to match. Sentences seen verbatim in training are excluded from validation and test; intervals resample test jobs rather than treating every sentence as an independent advertisement.

03 · Put model scores in context

Current reruns: fair_tfidf: 0.7272; cnn: 0.3679; minilm: 0.6705. TF-IDF uses full descriptions; CNN and MiniLM use truncated inputs. The three-epoch CNN and frozen encoder are budgeted comparisons, not exhaustive model tuning.

04 · What would change a practical decision?

Retain the text baseline for broad-category routing until a new feature design clears a relevant gain criterion. Use extracted requirements as source-linked candidates for human review, not as automatic judgments about a person's competence or suitability.

A next study could filter generic terms using training data, tune fusion weights on validation data, and reserve a new employer/time holdout for one final comparison. Those experiments have not been performed here.

Evidence and limits

The downloader rebuilds the archived cohort from fixed public releases and checks 22,213 source references. The selection ledger preserves the delivered membership; it does not recover the missing original split-generation script. Historical English advertisements do not represent current US, Singapore or Hong Kong hiring.

The paper reports an exploratory JobStreet fusion decline, but its full CSV and archived exclusion index were not supplied and its card has conflicting licenses. This release does not claim to reproduce that external experiment.
